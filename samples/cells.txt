A cell is the smallest unit of life. Every living thing is made of one or more cells. The nucleus is the control centre of the cell and holds the instructions for making proteins. The cell membrane is a thin skin around the cell that controls what enters and leaves. Inside the cell, the cytoplasm is a jelly-like substance where most chemical reactions happen. A human body has about 37 trillion cells, and a single cell is about 10 micrometres across.
