Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their own food. Inside the chloroplasts, chlorophyll absorbs sunlight. The plant uses this energy to turn carbon dioxide from the air and water from the soil into glucose and oxygen. The overall reaction is 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2, which requires light. Oxygen is released through tiny pores called stomata, while glucose is used for growth or stored as starch.
