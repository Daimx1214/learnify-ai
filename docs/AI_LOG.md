## What no AI did

- No AI service other than Pollinations runs inside the product. `PROVIDER = 'pollinations'` (free, no key); the passage is sent to Pollinations only when AI mode is on, nothing is stored by Samajh, and AI mode can be switched off for a fully offline run.
- The AI never writes quiz questions or glossary translations: those are always built offline from the passage and the checked seed glossary, so no AI-invented answer or translation can appear.
- No student test was run, so no student quotes or ratings are claimed anywhere.
- No statistics, citations or external facts were added. Everything measured is in `eval/results.md`.
