Privacy & AI — read this before your first use

No accounts. Nothing is stored. Your text stays in this browser tab and disappears when you close it. Do not paste personal information (your name, phone number, address).

AI mode (on by default): the explanation is written by a free AI (Pollinations text API, no key, no sign-up). Your passage is sent to that service to be explained — nothing is stored by Samajh, and no account is needed. Before it is shown, Samajh checks the AI text: if it changes any number or formula from your passage, the AI explanation is thrown away and the offline one is shown instead. The quiz and glossary are always built offline, from your own passage and a checked glossary, so the AI can never invent a quiz answer or a translation.

Offline mode: switch AI mode off and the app runs on a rule-based offline engine — it re-uses sentences from your own passage, sends nothing anywhere, and the explanation stays in English. Do not paste personal information (your name, phone number, address).

It can be wrong. Check any quiz item marked “Could not verify — check yourself”. Samajh is a study helper, not a replacement for your teacher or textbook.

How it works

1

Paste

Paste a STEM paragraph, or tap a sample. Pick your level and language.

2

Understand

In AI mode, a free AI explains the paragraph in your chosen language. Samajh checks that explanation against your passage, then lists the key terms and builds the quiz offline.

3

Test yourself

Answer five questions. Every answer shows the exact sentence from your passage that proves it — and the app checks that the sentence really is there.

Trust & limits

What Samajh does not do

  • It is not a teacher and not a textbook. If a badge says “Could not verify — check yourself”, check the passage yourself.
  • In offline mode it cannot translate a whole passage into your language — it re-uses your English sentences and gives the glossary and quiz in your chosen language. In AI mode the explanation is written in your chosen language.
  • It does not store anything, does not know who you are, and does not work on photos or handwriting.
  • New-language glossary meanings (beyond Urdu and Roman Urdu) are standard dictionary translations of common STEM terms — they have not yet been reviewed by native speakers.

About & AI

Samajh was built by Daim Ali, a university student in Multan, for the STEM2Connect AI Hackathon 2026 (Education track), between 11 and 21 November 2026.

AI disclosure. Inside the product there is a real AI feature: the explanation and one-line takeaway are written by the Pollinations text API (model “openai”, free, no API key, no sign-up), tested live on 21 November 2026. Before the AI text is shown, Samajh checks it — any number or formula not in the passage discards it and the offline explanation is used instead. The quiz and glossary are always built offline by the rule-based engine (extractive sentences, a checked seed glossary, cloze questions), so the AI never invents a quiz answer or a translation. If the AI call fails, the offline engine takes over automatically. An AI coding assistant (Muse) helped write the code; the code was run and tested before submission, the quiz and explanation bugs the tests found were fixed, and the sample passages were written for this project. The full log is in docs/AI_LOG.md.
